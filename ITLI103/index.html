Privacy Policy

BloodConnect Donor Data & Trust Guidelines

At BloodConnect, we are committed to maintaining your trust and protecting your personal details when you choose to join our life-saving donor network.

How Your Data Is Used

When you register as a donor, your information (including your name, phone number, blood group, city, and PIN code) will be used and accessed specifically by individuals or families in urgent need of blood donations during medical emergencies.

Key Highlights

Purpose-Driven Access: Your contact details are made available to users searching for blood donors matching your specific blood group and location.
Direct Emergency Contact: Patients or their representatives will use your provided phone number solely to reach out to you regarding urgent blood donation requests.
No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to marketing agencies or unauthorized third parties.
Voluntary Participation: Registering as a donor is completely voluntary, and you may request to update or remove your details from our active search directory at any time.

By registering on BloodConnect, you consent to sharing your contact information with recipients and patients searching for emergency blood donors.

Platform Guidelines & User Responsibilities

1 Voluntary Platform

BloodConnect is an independent, non-commercial directory designed to connect voluntary blood donors with individuals in urgent need during medical emergencies. We do not operate as a commercial blood bank or healthcare facility.

2 Donor Responsibilities & Eligibility

  • Accurate Information: Donors agree to provide truthful, accurate, and up-to-date contact and location details during registration.
  • Health Eligibility: Donors must ensure they meet standard medical guidelines and local eligibility criteria for safe blood donation before donating.
  • Voluntary Consent: Donors register freely with full understanding that their contact information will be made visible to patients or families searching for emergency blood donors.

3 Recipient Responsibilities

  • Legitimate Emergency Use: Requesters agree to use donor contact details strictly for emergency blood donation requests.
  • Respectful Communication: Requesters must communicate respectfully and refrain from spamming, harassing, or inappropriately contacting registered donors.

  • No Direct Medical Advice: BloodConnect does not provide medical services, medical screening, or health guarantees.

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Commercializing, selling, or purchasing blood or related services through this platform.
  • Using donor details for commercial marketing, solicitation, or any non-emergency purposes.
  • Submitting false, fraudulent, or malicious donor listings or search requests.

6 Limitation of Liability

BloodConnect and its administrators shall not be held liable for any damages, losses, health complications, or disputes arising from communications, agreements, or medical procedures conducted between donors and recipients.
